# Platinum Neue notes
- Raised means pressable. Inset means editable.
- Accent only for selection, focus, default
button and progress. Nothing else.
- Pinstripes say "grab here", and only on
the window that has focus.
- Shade is one double-click away.
- Toggles look pressed in when on.
## Decided
- Close button: neutral, faint warm tint.
- Pinstripes: three grooves, 4px apart.
- Serif: panel clock and tray popups only.
- Depth: quiet. Edges and surfaces, no gloss.

    If you don’t save, the changes you made in the last 12 minutes will be lost.
    Why these choices

    The destructive option stays on the far left, away from Save. The default button is the only filled control in the window. The alert glyph is drawn as an outline in text colour, so colour isn't needed to read it.

    From Platinum to Neue

    What each Platinum idea was for, and how it carries over.

    Bevels: raised and inset

    Raised still means you can press it, and inset still means you can type in it. The heavy bevels become a 1px border, a highlight along the top edge and a soft shadow underneath. Fields have a darker top edge.

    Grey chrome, white content

    Two surfaces carry the hierarchy, which means fewer lines and shadows are needed. Sidebars are chrome, lists and documents are content.

    Pinstriped titlebar

    Pinstripes appear only on the free space beside the title, and only on the focused window. Inactive windows go flat, so you can tell which window has focus without relying on colour.

    WindowShade

    Double-clicking a titlebar collapses the window to its title, everywhere. The Platinum button layout (close on the left, zoom and shade on the right) is a preset.

    One highlight colour

    The accent is used only for selection, focus, the default button, progress and menu highlight. Toggles show they're on by looking pressed in, not by turning blue.

    Fixed, legible metrics

    A 4px grid, 24px controls and permanent scrollbars with a grip. Every control has a 2px focus ring for keyboard use. Text contrast is at least 7:1, and control borders are at least 3:1.

    Live values for the current mode and accent. These become tokens.json once approved.

    Type

    Bricolage Grotesque is used for everything. At UI sizes, optical sizing tightens it up. EB Garamond is used only in the panel clock and tray popups.

    EB GARAMOND · RESERVE FACE
    Every interface is a set of promises about what will happen when you touch it.

    Platinum kept its promises with bevels and stripes. This theme keeps them with fewer marks: raised things press, inset things accept text, and the grooved bar at the top of the focused window is where you take hold of it.
